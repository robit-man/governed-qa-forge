## Executive summary

The paper demonstrates the value of constrained, pedagogically styled synthetic data for a focused capability domain rather than indiscriminate web-like generation.

## Method and findings

It curates textbook-quality code and exercises, trains small models, and evaluates coding capability. Clear exposition and concept progression are treated as generation requirements.

## Relevance and limitations

The evidence is code-specific and details are not fully reproducible. “Textbook-like” style can also conceal factual error unless grounded.

## Implementation implication

Add pedagogy dimensions—prerequisites, difficulty, explanation form, misconception targeted—but verify every factual or executable answer.
