## Executive summary

This study compares diversity measures and uses determinantal point processes to select varied subsets, reinforcing that record count is a poor coverage proxy.

## Method and findings

Lexical and embedding-space signals capture different forms of diversity. DPP-style selection rewards quality while penalizing redundancy.

## Relevance and limitations

Use multiple diversity views—taxonomy occupancy, lexical variation, embedding neighborhoods, and source-family concentration. Embedding distance is representation-dependent and can mistake superficial wording for substantive difference.

## Implementation implication

Publish diversity dashboards and calibrate semantic thresholds on labeled duplicate/non-duplicate pairs from this corpus.
