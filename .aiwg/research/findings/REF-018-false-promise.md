## Executive summary

Imitation data can transfer conversational style without transferring the teacher’s underlying factual knowledge or robust capability.

## Method and findings

The study compares broad evaluations of models trained on teacher outputs and identifies a gap between surface behavior and task competence.

## Relevance and limitations

Synthetic Q/A should teach verifiable operations and grounded knowledge rather than optimize for plausible voice. The study does not reject all distillation; it rejects treating imitation volume as capability evidence.

## Implementation implication

Require answer support, executable checks where possible, and external capability evaluations not authored or graded by the generating teacher.
