## Executive summary

Persona-conditioned generation can broaden task framing and user perspective, with zero-shot and few-shot modes providing different diversity controls.

## Method and findings

The work constructs a very large persona collection and applies lexical and embedding deduplication to generated tasks.

## Relevance and limitations

Persona count is not coverage, and fabricated identities can amplify stereotypes or encourage inferred sensitive attributes. Personas should describe interaction context or expertise, not synthetic demographic claims.

## Implementation implication

Use a bounded, reviewed persona taxonomy with safety constraints and measure whether each persona changes task substance rather than wording alone.
