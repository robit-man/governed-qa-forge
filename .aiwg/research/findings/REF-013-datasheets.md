## Executive summary

Datasheets make motivation, composition, collection, preprocessing, uses, distribution, and maintenance explicit across the dataset lifecycle.

## Method and findings

The framework supplies structured questions that surface assumptions and responsibilities often lost when a dataset is reduced to rows and a license label.

## Relevance and limitations

Every corpus release and major category family should have a versioned data card/datasheet. Documentation increases auditability but does not prove permission, safety, representativeness, or correctness.

## Implementation implication

Make release-card completion and hash inclusion mandatory before promotion from candidate to released corpus.
